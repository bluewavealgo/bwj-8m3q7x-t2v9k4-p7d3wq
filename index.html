BlueWave Algo is an independent algo-trading channel from India. We design, test and run rule-based options strategies, so trades are placed by code instead of emotion.

Every strategy runs fully automated on Tradetron and places its trades on NSE F&O (NIFTY options) and Delta Exchange India (Bitcoin and Ethereum options). Positions are hedged spreads, so the maximum risk on each trade is defined before entry.

We publish backtest results month by month, losing months included, and share strategy updates and learnings with our community on Telegram.
