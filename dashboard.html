About these numbers

  • HurricaneX: backtest 1 Jan 2024 to 31 Oct 2026. ETH Polar Wave: 1 Jan 2024 to 16 Oct 2026. Typhoon Shield v1: 1 Jan 2022 to 10 Sep 2026. Current Rider v1: 1 Jan 2022 to 29 Sep 2026.
  • CAGR and drawdown are Tradetron's net-of-costs figures over each full backtest window. All other P&L figures are gross.
  • Tradetron flags all four runs as partial coverage: some sessions or strikes had no data, so totals cover slightly less than the full window.
  • Backtest results are hypothetical and do not guarantee future returns. Not investment advice; not SEBI-registered.
